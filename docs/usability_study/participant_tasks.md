# Tasks

Please do the tasks in order and think aloud. You may give up on any task.

---

### T1. Start of shift

You have just started your shift. Using the dashboard, find out:
- how many items currently need your attention;
- which area (cyber, safety, physical security, maintenance or environment) has the most abnormal results.

---

### T2. The most urgent group

Find the group of related alerts with the **highest severity**. Tell us:
- its severity;
- which areas its alerts come from.

---

### T3. Why are these together?

Open that group. In your own words, explain **why the system put these alerts together**, and say whether the system claims that one alert *caused* another.

---

### T4. Check a detector result

Find a **safety-equipment (PPE) result** that nobody has reviewed yet. Look at it and record your own judgement, agreeing or disagreeing with the detector, with a short reason.

---

### T5. Work on an investigation

Take one of the **correlated groups** that is still open:
- record that you are now reviewing it;
- then close it, choosing the outcome you think fits, and give a short reason.

---

### T6. What is still waiting?

Find the place that lists the work still **waiting for human review**. Choose one item and open its details.

---

### T7. A summary for your manager

Your manager asks which **zone** has produced the most evidence. Find the answer.

---

### T8 (optional). Ask the assistant

Open the group from T2 and use the **AI Investigator** to ask: *"Why is this group high priority?"* Then tell us whether you trust the answer and why.

---

### T9 (optional). Check a camera image

In the Scenario Lab, upload the image the facilitator gives you (`camera_snapshot.jpg`) as a new **PPE image** for zone ZONE-B. Tell us what the system concluded about the person in it.

---

### T10 (optional). Add new sensor readings

In the Scenario Lab, add the sensor file the facilitator gives you (`sensor_readings.csv`) for sensor **SEN-B-01** in zone **ZONE-B**. Then find the new result in the Monitoring Center and tell us what the system found.
