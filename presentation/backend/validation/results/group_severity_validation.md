## Reading

On the test split the live rule forms 17 groups; 3 are HIGH, of which 2 are one genuine incident. Severity here depends only on which domains are present (three or more domains give HIGH), so a coincidental group spanning many domains is rated as severe as a real multi-domain incident. Treat group severity as provisional until severity labels exist; a severity column could be added to the independent labelling pack.
