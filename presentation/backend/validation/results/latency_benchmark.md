## Caveats

- One workstation, one user, small data: no concurrency or load test; production latency depends on hosting.
- Inference runs in a new worker process per upload (isolation of the component environments); a long-running model service would remove most of the model-loading time.
- Observed language-model latency depends on the provider and network at the time, and the specialist routes have few samples (each call needs operator consent).
- The access logs rotate, so part B covers only the period shown.
